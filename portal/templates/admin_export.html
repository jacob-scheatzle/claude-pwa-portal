Export your data

A portable .zip of your business's own data — open data.json in any tool, or move it to another system. No secrets, no lock-in.

What's included

  • data.json — your apps (with their tool & form definitions), users (email and role only), every public-form submission, your scheduled runs, and the audit log.
  • storage/ — every app's per-user stored files, laid out as storage/<app>/<user-id>/<key>.

What's not included

  • Password hashes, API token hashes, SMTP credentials, and SECRET_KEY — this file is safe to share and store in ordinary places.
Need a full restore instead (database + secrets)? Use the Backup page — that snapshot is for disaster recovery and does contain encrypted secrets.
